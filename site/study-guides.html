Study Guides

Turn your messy class notes into something you can actually study from: summaries, flashcard questions, practice quizzes, and a study checklist.

Step by step

  1. Copy your lecture notes (type them up first if they’re handwritten).
  2. Paste them into a free AI chat.
  3. Ask for a short summary in plain language.
  4. Ask for flashcard-style questions: “Give me 8 Q&A pairs I can quiz myself with.”
  5. Ask for a practice quiz: multiple choice or short answer, with an answer key at the end.
  6. Ask for a study checklist: “What should I review before the test?”
  7. Copy the results into a document or notebook and add your own notes.

Example prompts you can copy

Here are my notes from [class name]: [paste notes]. Can you write a short
summary in plain language, like you're explaining it to a friend?
Based on these notes, give me 8 flashcard-style questions and answers. Put the
question on one line and the answer on the next.
Make a 10-question practice quiz from these notes. Include an answer key at the
end, and mark which questions are easy, medium, or hard.
What are the 5 most important things from these notes that I should review
before the test? Make it a checklist I can print.

Privacy check: Do not paste grades, GPA, or score reports. Do not paste your student ID number. Remove your name and your classmates' names from notes before pasting.

Tip for low-energy days

If you’re tired (ADHD-friendly tip), do one small piece at a time: paste a few paragraphs of notes, ask for one summary, then take a break. You don’t have to finish it all in one sitting.
