Printable Study Planner

A one-page weekly study planner you can print from your browser. It has space for Monday–Friday time blocks, classes and work shifts, study sessions and breaks, assignments due this week, and a “one thing to focus on” note.

How to print

  1. Open this page (study-planner.html) in any web browser.
  2. Press Ctrl+P (or Cmd+P on Mac) to open the print dialog.
  3. Choose “Save as PDF” or send it to your printer.
  4. Turn on “Background graphics” in the print settings so colors show up (optional — it prints fine in black and white too).
Print a fresh copy every Sunday. Fill in your fixed commitments first, then add study blocks around them. Leave blank space for things that come up.

Low-energy / ADHD-friendly use

  • Plan just one day at a time if a full week feels like too much.
  • Write down only the “must-do” tasks, then add nice-to-haves if you have energy.
  • Cross things off as you finish them — seeing progress helps.
