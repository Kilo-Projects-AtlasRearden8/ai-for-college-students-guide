Essay Brainstorming

Staring at a blank essay prompt is normal. AI can help you break the ice by suggesting angles, questions, and loose outlines — not writing the essay for you. You still do the thinking and the writing.

Step by step

  1. Open a free AI chat tool in your browser (for example, a free model hosted by your college library, or any free-tier AI assistant you already use).
  2. Copy your essay prompt and paste it into the chat.
  3. Ask the AI to suggest 3–5 different angles or questions you could explore.
  4. Pick the one that interests you most and ask for a loose outline (introduction idea, 2–3 body points, conclusion idea).
  5. Use the outline as a starting point, then change it however you want.
  6. Write the essay yourself in your own words.

Example prompts you can copy

I have this essay prompt: [paste your prompt]. Can you suggest 4 different
angles or questions I could write about? Keep it simple.
Here's one angle I like: [describe it]. Can you make a loose outline with an
introduction idea, three body paragraphs, and a conclusion idea? Use bullet
points.
I need to write a [number]-page essay for my [class name] class. What are
some questions I should try to answer in the paper?

Privacy check: Do not paste your grades, GPA, or past scored essays with your name on them. Do not paste your student ID number. If you paste a classmate’s essay or draft, get their permission first.

Remember: AI gives you a starting point. The ideas, examples, and words in your final essay should be yours. Check your school’s rules about AI use before you start.
