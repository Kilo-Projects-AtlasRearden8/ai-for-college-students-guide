# AI for College Students — Getting Started

Welcome. This manual walks you through using free AI tools for college work:
brainstorming essays, making study guides, getting citation help, planning your
schedule, and drafting emails. It's written plain, step by step, like a friend
would explain it. No jargon required.

## Start here: privacy

Before you type anything into an AI tool, read
[docs/privacy.md](privacy.md). The short version: never paste grades, GPA,
student ID numbers, or anything you wouldn't want posted on a campus bulletin
board.

## What "free AI" means here

The tools in this manual are free to use (or have generous free tiers). You
don't need to pay, and you don't need to share your credit card to get started.
Most of them work right in your web browser.

## How to use this manual

Each chapter follows the same pattern:

1. **What it's for** — when you'd use this in real college life.
2. **Step by step** — exactly what to click and type.
3. **Example prompts** — copy, paste, and edit these.
4. **Privacy check** — what to keep out of the AI chat.

Pick one chapter and try it. You don't need to read the whole thing first.
