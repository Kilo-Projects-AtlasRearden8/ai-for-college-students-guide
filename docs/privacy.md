# Privacy Rules

Read this before you paste anything into an AI tool.

## The golden rule

Treat every AI chat as if it's public. Don't type anything you wouldn't feel
comfortable writing on a campus bulletin board.

## Never paste these

1. **Grades, GPA, or score reports.** Never paste your grades, GPA, individual
   assignment scores, or transcripts into an AI tool.
2. **Student ID numbers.** Never paste your student ID number, university ID,
   or any government ID number (Social Security number, etc.).
3. **Your full name combined with school details.** Avoid pasting your full
   name together with your class schedule, major, or college name.
4. **Classmates' work or names.** Never paste a classmate's essay, notes, or
   personal information without their explicit permission.
5. **Passwords, financial info, or health records.** Never paste passwords,
   bank info, insurance details, or medical records.

## What's generally okay to paste

- Essay prompts and assignment instructions (without your name or ID attached).
- Your own class notes (with names removed).
- Citation details for published sources.
- General questions like "How do I write a thesis statement?"

## If you need to include personal info

Use a placeholder instead, then fill it in yourself:

```
[Student ID]  instead of your real ID
[My name]     instead of your real name
[Professor name] instead of a real name
```

## Why this matters

AI chats may be stored, reviewed by people, or used to train future models.
Once something is pasted, you can't take it back. Protect your school data the
same way you protect your passwords.

## Your school's rules

Check your college's AI policy. Some instructors allow AI for brainstorming but
not for writing drafts; others have different rules. When in doubt, ask your
professor.
