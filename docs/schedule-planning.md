# Schedule Planning

## What it's for

Use AI to turn your class schedule, work shifts, and assignment due dates into a
weekly plan you can actually follow. The AI makes the draft; you adjust it until
it fits your life.

## Step by step

1. List your fixed commitments: class times, work shifts, study blocks, sleep,
   meals.
2. List your assignments and due dates.
3. Paste both lists into a free AI chat.
4. Ask for a weekly schedule: "Make me a Monday–Friday schedule with study
   blocks, breaks, and time for sleep."
5. Ask the AI to flag anything that looks too packed: "Did I schedule enough
   breaks? Is any day overloaded?"
6. Copy the schedule into your phone calendar or a printed planner.
7. Try it for one week, then adjust what didn't work.

## Example prompts you can copy

```
Here are my classes and work shifts: [paste them]. Here are my assignments and
due dates: [paste them]. Can you make me a realistic Monday–Friday schedule
with study blocks, breaks, meals, and 7–8 hours of sleep?
```

```
I have [number] hours free this week to study for [class]. Break it into
30–60 minute blocks with breaks between them.
```

```
Look at my schedule above. Did I leave enough time for breaks and sleep? Is any
day too packed? Suggest one change.
```

## Printable planner

There's a printable weekly study planner at `site/planner.html`. Open it in your
browser and print (or save to PDF). It fits on one sheet of paper.

## Privacy check

- Do NOT paste your student ID number.
- Do NOT paste grades.
- Your class times and due dates are generally fine, but avoid pasting anything
  that identifies you personally (like your full name with your schedule).

## Low-energy tip

If a full weekly plan feels like too much, plan just three days at a time. Or
plan only the "must-do" items and leave the rest flexible.
