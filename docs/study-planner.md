# Printable Study Planner

## What it is

A one-page weekly study planner you can print from your browser. It has space
for:

- Monday–Friday time blocks
- Classes and work shifts
- Study sessions and breaks
- Assignments due this week
- A "one thing to focus on" note

## How to print

1. Open `site/planner.html` in any web browser.
2. Press Ctrl+P (or Cmd+P on Mac) to open the print dialog.
3. Choose "Save as PDF" or send it to your printer.
4. Make sure "Background graphics" is turned on in the print settings so the
   colors show up (optional — it prints fine in black and white too).

## Tip

Print a fresh copy every Sunday. Fill in your fixed commitments first, then add
study blocks around them. Leave blank space for things that come up.

## Low-energy / ADHD-friendly use

- Plan just one day at a time if a full week feels like too much.
- Write down only the "must-do" tasks, then add nice-to-haves if you have
  energy.
- Cross things off as you finish them — seeing progress helps.
